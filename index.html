"What is SmileBucks?"

SmileBucks is a currency that we are trying to spread across schools, soon this website will get future updates with images of Smile Bucks put on here but for now there won't.

"How does the banking of SmileBucks work?"

The banking of SmileBucks works by having a day per week called a "Pay Day" which allows you to earn SmileBucks every month. If you were here when SmileBucks was first created, you would know that jobs used to exist. The jobs were actually canceled because people were not being consisent with their job, so only the managers can work jobs/sell things but you still can earn money FROM the pay days.

"What if i do want a job and promise to stay consisent?"

If you want a job and PROMISE that you will stay consistent, you can get a job but only for certain days on a week, because we also canceled jobs because recess is a time where kids get to have fun and relax/not work. So we don't want to overwork kids with their jobs so only if you do abouslutely promise to stay consistent, then you can get a job.

"Why was SmileBucks made?"

Well, we made SmileBucks because we wanted to make a currency that people could use to earn and spend, like just simply buying snacks to eat during lunch, or buying some stationery for class! We also just thought it would be fun to make a currency for our school!

"Can I write for the daily newspaper?"

No, you sadly can't write for the daily newspaper, but what you can do is give suggestions for the newsletterrs by emailing me! (email at luna@smilebucks.org)

"Can i spread the word even if i'm not from this school?"

Yes! you can spread the word even if you're not from this school! You can just tell your other friends about this website or tell them about Smile Bucks and that your school should start using the currency!
